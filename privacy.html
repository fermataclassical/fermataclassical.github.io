Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Fermata is a personal hobby project used only by its owner. It does not collect any data from visitors or other users, uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

What is accessed: after the owner logs in with the owner's own TikTok account (and the owner's own accounts on other platforms), the project reads that account's basic profile information, follower count, and the view, like and comment counts of its own videos.

How it is used: these numbers are shown on a personal dashboard that runs on the owner's own computer and is not public. They are statistics of the owner's own account, of the kind that are also visible on the public account. The dashboard contains no data about other users.

Processing: the connection to the platforms is handled through the integration service Composio (composio.dev), which stores the access authorization for the owner's account and retrieves the statistics on the owner's behalf.

Sharing: the data is not shared with anyone, and never sold.

Deleting data: the owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app settings and in Composio, and delete the stored statistics.
