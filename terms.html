Fermata is a personal hobby project around classical music (@fermata.classical). It consists of a small personal dashboard, used only by the owner on the owner's own computer, to view the statistics of the owner's own social media accounts, such as follower counts and video views.

The dashboard is not public and is not offered as a service to other people. There is no sign-up, no user accounts and no way for visitors to connect their own accounts. Only the owner connects accounts, by logging in with the owner's own account and giving permission.

The project connects to platform APIs (such as TikTok's) only for reading statistics. It does not post content, comment, send messages, or act on behalf of anyone else.

The project is provided as is, without any warranty. The owner can stop the project or remove access at any time.
